About Us

Zanele Zaynab Nkutha, the founder of ROOTD in UBUNTU, suffered from chronic migraines for years. Conventional medicine was ineffective and expensive. She turned to traditional remedies used by our ancestors, but finding them online was difficult – which inspired her to create this platform.

In 2025, ROOTD in UBUNTU was established for those seeking help through traditional remedies as an alternative when conventional medicines are not effective. Our story is just beginning. We are building a future where South African traditional medicine is the global benchmark for holistic health.

Mission

To provide standardised, high‑quality traditional remedies designed for a modern lifestyle.

Vision

To bridge the gap between African heritage and global health by becoming a well‑known source of African botanical wellness.

Our Team

Our team is a blend of herbalists with deep knowledge of traditional remedies.
